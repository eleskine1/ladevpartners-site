§ 004 · THE PRACTICE
We are the considered middle, between client and craft, keeping the whole of a project held together from first sketch through final walk-through.

LA Development Partners is a Los Angeles construction management practice. We guide owners, developers, and institutions through the making of buildings: feasibility, design, permitting, pricing, construction, and the handover.

Our partnered roster of builders, architects, engineers, landscape and interior designers joins each engagement under our direction, so the result feels inevitable. The practice is led by its principal, who has been building in Los Angeles since 2020.
